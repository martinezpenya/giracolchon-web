How it works

Three steps and you can forget the calendar

  1. 1

    Add your mattress

    Name, size and how it is turned: head to foot only, with flipping, or with a winter and a summer side.

  2. 2

    Get the reminder

    Every week, every month or when the season changes, at the time you choose. If you miss it, it reminds you the next day.

  3. 3

    Turn it and confirm

    An animation shows you which move is next. Confirm it and it goes into the history.

Everything a mattress needs to last longer

Reminders at your time

A different reminder time for each mattress and a daily reminder if you do not confirm.

Animated turning and flipping

The app suggests the next move to spread wear across the four positions.

Winter and summer sides

For two-sided mattresses, it reminds you a few days before each change of season.

Warranty and lifespan

Keep the receipt and warranty, and get a reminder before it expires.

Statistics

How many times you have turned each mattress, how punctually and how often it has been in each position.

Backups

In a ZIP you can encrypt and keep in your cloud: Drive, OneDrive or Nextcloud.

Every mattress with its own drawing

The drawing follows each mattress's size. Choose the headboard shape and colours to tell the double bed from the guest bed at a glance.

Privacy

Your data stays on your phone

No accounts, no sign-up. The app works offline and does not send your data to any server.
